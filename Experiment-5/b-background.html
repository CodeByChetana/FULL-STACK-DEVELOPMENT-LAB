🖼️ CSS Background Properties

background-image: Loads the background image.

background-repeat: no-repeat; Prevents the image from repeating.

background-position: center 50%; Positions it horizontally in the center and vertically halfway down the background area.

background-attachment: fixed; Keeps the background stationary as the page scrolls.

background-size: cover; Scales the image to cover the background area.
